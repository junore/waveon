Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

In short: WaveOn doesn't collect personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing is sold or shared. What you keep stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your favorite stations, followed podcasts and episode progress, downloaded episodes, song history, equalizer settings and other preferences are stored only on your device, inside the app. They aren't sent to us. You can export them to a file (Settings → Your Data) and delete them at any time, for instance by removing the app.

Services the app contacts

To work, the app connects directly to these services. As with any internet connection, they receive your device's IP address and the request; none of them receives an identifier, name or email of yours.

  • Radio Browser (radio-browser.info): the station directory. It receives station searches and the chosen country.
  • The radio stations themselves: live audio comes straight from each station's servers.
  • Apple (iTunes Search API and podcast charts): podcast search and charts, and album covers looked up from the title of the song playing.
  • Last.fm: album covers, from the song title, when Apple has none.
  • images.weserv.nl: an image service used to show some station logos securely (HTTPS).
  • Each podcast's host: feeds and episodes are downloaded straight from their publishers.
  • Apple Music, Spotify and YouTube Music: only when you choose “Open in Apple Music” (or Spotify, YouTube Music) on a song in the history, the app opens a search for that song in that service.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

Notifications

If you turn on “Notify when the song changes”, notifications are created on the device itself. We don't use push notifications or servers of our own.

Children

The app doesn't collect data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with the date it takes effect.
